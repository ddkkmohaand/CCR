Assumptions and limitations
  • Borrowers, loans and valuations are fictional.
  • Hazard values and risk zones are simulated demonstration inputs. No external hazard data source is used.
  • Scores come from transparent demonstration rules, not validated models or flood forecasts.
  • Locations are approximate district centres with small offsets. They are not survey-verified or checked against land boundaries.
  • Unknown data is never scored as low risk. Not Evaluated assets carry no risk category.
  • Loan exposure is the outstanding fictional loan. Collateral valuation is the estimated property value. They are different measures.
